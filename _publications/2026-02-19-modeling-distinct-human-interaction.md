This work introduces the task of modeling human intervention for collaborative web task execution. It collects CowCorpus, a dataset of 400 real-user web navigation trajectories with over 4,200 interleaved human and agent actions, identifies distinct user interaction patterns, and trains language models to anticipate likely interventions.
