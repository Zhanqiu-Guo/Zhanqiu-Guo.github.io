ContextWIN extends Neural Whittle Index Networks with a mixture-of-experts architecture for context-aware restless multi-armed bandits. The model uses contextual information to weight expert networks for Whittle index estimation, improving adaptive decision making in dynamic environments such as recommendation systems.
